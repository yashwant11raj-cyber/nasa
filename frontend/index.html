How to spot a moving object
  1. Click a test target below (try WISE 0855), or type coordinates and press Go.
  2. Press Play. Stars stay in the same place in every picture.
  3. Look for a dot that is in a different place from one frame to the next. That dot is moving.
  4. Slow down: pick Slow speed, or step one frame at a time with the left and right arrow keys.
  5. Try Difference view. Grey means no change. Things that moved show up as white or black spots.
  6. Open Advanced tools and use Blink to flip between the first and last frame. A mover seems to jump.
  7. Check Is it real? in Frame info. A yellow warning means the detector or colour changed, so a jump might not be real motion.
